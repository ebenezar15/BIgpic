Death destroyed through the body

Christ defeated death at the cross and the empty tomb. He now reigns until every enemy is under his feet, and his feet are his people. He is waiting, and so is creation, for the sons to be revealed.

    The six lenses

    Each one adds something to the picture: a reigning Christ, a fulfilled old covenant, a humanity united to him, a gift of life, a kingdom growing on earth, and a hope for all.

    Where other Christians read it differently

    Amillennial readers also see Christ reigning now and Revelation 20 as the present age, but expect no golden age of the gospel before his return. Premillennial and dispensational readers put Daniel’s seventieth week, the tribulation and a thousand-year reign after a future return. Full preterists place the second coming and the resurrection in AD 70 as well. Partial preterists, with the historic creeds, keep a final bodily return, resurrection and judgement still ahead.

    Traditional readers hold that union with Christ is received by faith and that judgement may be final. The hope of the sons in Romans 8 belongs to all God’s children. It is different from the Latter Rain “Manifest Sons of God” teaching (1948–51), which expected an elite group to gain immortality before Christ returns. Dominion here means reigning by service and the Spirit. It does not mean taking control of institutions.
